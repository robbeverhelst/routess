routess is open source and built to keep your data yours. The full privacy policy lives on [routess.com/privacy](https://routess.com/privacy); this page is the short version for app users.

## What routess stores

- Your name and email address (from Google sign-in or email signup)
- Your saved routes (waypoints and metadata such as name, activity, and visibility)

## Analytics

routess uses Umami, a privacy-friendly, cookie-less analytics tool, self-hosted on routess infrastructure. Usage events are anonymous: they never include your email, your route names, or your raw account ID. There are no third-party trackers and no advertising profiles.

## Your controls

- **Export**: download a full copy of your account (JSON + GPX per route) from your profile settings.
- **Visibility**: every route is private by default. You decide per route whether it's private, unlisted, or public.
- **Delete**: deleting your account removes your routes and profile after a 30-day grace window. See [Deleting your account](/en/guide/account/deleting-account).

## Self-hosting

If you run your own routess instance, your data stays on your infrastructure. There is no phone-home built in.
