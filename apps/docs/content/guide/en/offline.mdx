---
title: Offline & installing the app
description: Install routess as an app and use it with a poor connection.
---

routess is a progressive web app (PWA): you can install it from the browser and it keeps working when your connection drops, which is exactly when you're out on a trail.

## Install it

The easiest way is from inside routess: open **Settings** and use **Install app**. On most browsers that triggers the install prompt directly; on iOS it walks you through Safari's **Share → Add to Home Screen** steps.

Installing from the browser itself also works:

- **Chrome / Edge (desktop and Android)**: open the browser menu and choose **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
- **Safari (iOS)**: tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

The installed app opens in its own window, without browser chrome.

## What works offline

After your first visit, routess caches:

- the app itself, so it loads instantly even without a connection
- map tiles, styles, and fonts you've already viewed
- recent route calculations and location searches

Areas you've planned in before stay browsable, and routes you've opened stay available offline across app updates. Anything new (unvisited map areas, fresh route calculations, saving to your library) needs a connection; if a save can't reach the server, routess tells you rather than pretending it worked.

## Updates

The app updates itself in the background. When a new version is ready, a toast offers **Reload**; the update applies when you confirm or on the next fresh start, never mid-edit.
