title: Offline & installing the app

---

routess is a progressive web app (PWA): you can install it from the browser and it keeps working when your connection drops, which is exactly when you're out on a trail.

## Install it

- **Chrome / Edge (desktop and Android)**: open the browser menu and choose **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
- **Safari (iOS)**: tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

The installed app opens in its own window, without browser chrome.

## What works offline

After your first visit, routess caches:

- the app itself, so it loads instantly even without a connection
- map tiles, styles, and fonts you've already viewed
- recent route calculations and location searches

Areas you've planned in before stay browsable. Anything new (unvisited map areas, fresh route calculations, saving to your library) needs a connection and syncs once you're back online.

## Updates

The app updates itself in the background. When a new version is ready it's applied on the next reload; if something looks stale, reload the page once.
