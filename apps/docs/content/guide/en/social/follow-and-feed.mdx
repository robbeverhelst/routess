title: Follow & feed
description: Follow other people and see their newly published routes.
---

Open **Social** from the left rail (or bottom tab bar). It has three tabs: **Feed**, **Inbox**, and **Following**. All of them need an account.

![The Social feed listing routes published by people you follow](/guide/feed.jpg)

## Following someone

In the **Following** tab, search people by name or handle and click **Follow**. Following is one-directional and immediate: no approval step, and they don't have to follow you back. Unfollow any time from the same tab or from their profile.

## The feed

The **Feed** tab lists routes published by the people you follow, newest first. A route enters the feed when it first becomes **public**; toggling visibility back and forth doesn't re-publish it, so nobody can spam the feed.

From a feed entry you can open the route, or jump to the publisher's profile.

## What following does not do

Following someone never grants access to their routes: you see exactly the routes any visitor to their profile sees, the public ones. To hand a specific route to a specific person, use the [inbox](/en/guide/social/inbox) instead.
