---
title: Sending routes to people
description: Share a route directly with another routess user.
---

Besides [share links](/en/guide/routes/sharing-routes), you can send a saved route straight to another person on routess. It lands in their **Inbox** in the Social panel.

![The Social inbox showing a route someone sent you, with save and read actions](/guide/inbox.jpg)

## Send a route

1. Open the route's share options (from the plan panel or the route's detail panel).
2. Pick **Send to a person** and search the recipient by name or handle.
3. Optionally add a short message.
4. Send.

Only unlisted and public routes can be sent. If the route is private, routess says so and offers **Make unlisted & send**, which flips the visibility in the same step.

## Your inbox

The **Inbox** tab shows routes people sent you, with an unread badge on the Social icon. For each entry you can:

- **View** the route on the map
- **Save a copy** into your own library; your copy is private and fully yours
- Mark it read, or remove it

An inbox entry is a live reference, not a snapshot: if the sender later makes the route private or deletes it, the entry stops being viewable. Save a copy if you want to keep it for good.
