Every routess account has a **profile**: a public page showing your name, avatar, and your **public routes only**. Private and unlisted routes never appear on it, no matter who is looking.

![A public profile page with stats, follower counts, and public routes](/guide/profile.jpg)

## Your handle

Your handle is your unique name on routess, generated from your display name when the feature launched (never from your email). It's lowercase letters, digits, and hyphens, 3 to 30 characters.

Your profile lives at `app.routess.com/u/your-handle`. You can change the handle in **Settings**; note that links to the old handle stop working, and the freed handle becomes available to others.

## What's on a profile

- Name, handle, and avatar
- Stats computed over public routes: route count, total distance
- Follower and following counts
- The list of public routes

Anyone can view a profile, signed in or not. Follower **counts** are public; the actual list of who follows whom is visible only to you on your own profile.

## Sharing your profile

Just send the URL. To get routes onto it, set their visibility to **public** in the [route details](/en/guide/library/route-details).
