routess is a route planning app that lets you sketch a route on a map, waypoint by waypoint, and save it for later. This guide is for **people using the routess app**: no coding required.

![The routess planner with a route through Ghent](/guide/route-overview.jpg)

## What you can do

- Sign in with Google or email and start planning routes in seconds
- Click on the map to drop waypoints, drag to reorder, undo if you change your mind
- See total distance and estimated duration update live as you edit
- Save routes to your library, organise them with tags and collections
- Follow friends, see what they publish, and send routes to each other
- Import and export GPX, or install routess as an app and take it offline
- Switch the app to English, Dutch, French, or German
