## Sign-in problems

- **The Google popup is blocked.** Allow popups for the routess domain and try again.
- **"This account is not authorized."** routess uses your primary Google identity — try signing in from `accounts.google.com` first.
- **Stuck on a loading spinner after sign-in.** Reload the page. If it persists, clear cookies for the routess domain.

## Map won't load

- Check your internet connection
- Disable ad-blockers / privacy extensions for the routess domain — Mapbox tiles are sometimes blocked
- Try a different browser to rule out an extension issue

## My route disappeared

If you weren't signed in, routes live only in your browser and may have been cleared. Sign in next time to keep them.

## Location not working

See [Map → Your location](/en/guide/map/your-location).
