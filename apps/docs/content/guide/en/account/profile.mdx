Open **Settings** from the left rail to manage your profile and preferences.

![The settings panel with quick settings and sections](/guide/settings.jpg)

## What you can edit

- **Theme and units**: light or dark, metric or imperial (quick settings at the top)
- **Sports & pacing**: which sports you plan for (walking, running, cycling) and a default pace for each
- **Map & display**: map style, language, accent color, and map behavior
- **Privacy & sharing**: default visibility for new routes

Your display name lives under your account; email is read from your sign-in method and can't be changed in routess.

## Sports and pace

Pick one or more sports under **Sports & pacing** and mark one as your default for new routes. The currently-selected sport drives duration estimates on your routes. Each sport has its own default pace; override it if it doesn't match how fast you actually move. The change applies the next time the route is recalculated.
