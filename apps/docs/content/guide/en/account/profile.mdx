Open the menu in the top right and click your avatar to view your profile.

> _Screenshot placeholder: profile screen with sport settings._

## What you can edit

- Display name
- Distance unit (kilometers or miles)
- Default map style
- **Sports**: which sports you plan for (walking, running, cycling, driving) and a default pace for each

Email is read from your Google account and can't be changed in routess.

## Sports and pace

Pick one or more sports during onboarding, or change them later in **Settings → Sports**. The currently-selected sport drives duration estimates on your routes. Each sport has its own default pace; override it if it doesn't match how fast you actually move. The change applies the next time the route is recalculated.
