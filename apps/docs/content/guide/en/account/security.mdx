Open your account page from the avatar at the bottom of the left rail.

## Password

If you signed up with Google you can still **set a password**, so email + password works as a second way in. If you already have one, **change it** from the same place. Passwords must be at least 12 characters.

Forgot it? Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen; the reset link arrives by email.

## Sessions

The **Sessions** section shows this device. Signing out from the menu only ends the current session.

## Sign out of all devices

Under **Security**, **Sign out of all devices** revokes every active session, including the one you're using. Use it if you signed in on a shared computer or suspect someone else has access. You'll need to sign in again afterwards.

For scripted or third-party access to your library, use [API tokens](/en/guide/account/api-tokens) instead of your password.
