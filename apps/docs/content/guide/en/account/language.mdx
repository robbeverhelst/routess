title: Language
description: Change the app language.
---

routess is available in:

- English
- Nederlands
- Français
- Deutsch

![The language selector under Settings, Map & display](/guide/language.jpg)

Open **Settings** from the left rail, then **Map & display**. The **Language** selector sits under Appearance. The choice is remembered next time you visit.

## Want a language we don't have?

routess is open source and contributions are welcome. See [Developer Docs → Packages → i18n](/docs/packages/i18n) for how to add a locale.
