---
title: Deleting your account
description: Permanently delete your routess account and all your routes.
---

You can delete your routess account at any time. This is **permanent and irreversible**.

## What gets deleted

- Your profile
- All your saved routes
- Your sign-in association with Google

## How to delete

1. Open the menu → **Profile**
2. Scroll to **Danger zone**
3. Click **Delete account** and confirm

![The delete-account confirmation in the account Danger zone](/guide/delete-account.jpg)

After deletion, signing in with Google again creates a fresh account with no history.
