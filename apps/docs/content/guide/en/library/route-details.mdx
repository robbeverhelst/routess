---
title: Route details
description: The detail panel for a saved route, from rename to GPX export.
---

Click any card in your [library](/en/guide/library/your-library) to open the route's detail panel. The map preview stays behind it, so you see the route while you work.

## At the top

- **Name**: click the title to rename it in place.
- **Stat strip**: distance, duration, elevation gain, and average speed (cycling) or pace (running and walking).
- **Elevation chart**: the same elevation profile and surface breakdown as the planner.
- The header also holds **favourite** and **Save as copy** (duplicate).

## Properties

- **Activity**: run, cycle, or walk; switching re-estimates the duration.
- **Visibility**: private, unlisted, or public. See [Sharing routes](/en/guide/routes/sharing-routes) for what each means.
- **Tags**: add or remove tags; these drive the library's tag filter.

## Footer actions

- **Edit route**: open the route in the planner to change waypoints.
- **Share**: links, send to a person, and social targets.
- **Download GPX**: export the route as a GPX file.
- **Delete**: removes the route permanently.

Changing waypoints always happens in the planner; everything else about a route can be edited right here.
