---
title: Collections
description: Group routes into trips, training blocks, or wishlists.
---

Collections group saved routes into ordered lists: a cycling holiday, a training block, a "want to ride" wishlist. A route can be in any number of collections, and a collection only references routes, so removing a route from a collection never deletes the route itself.

![The Collections tab listing collections with their route counts](/guide/collections.jpg)

## Create a collection

Open the **Collections** tab in your library and click **New collection**. Type a name and press Enter.

## Add and arrange routes

Open a collection and use **Add routes** to pick from your library, or use **Add to collection** in a route card's menu from the Routes tab. Drag the grip handle on a row to reorder; on a phone, press and hold the handle, then drag.

## Share a collection

Use the collection's share action to copy a link. Sharing makes the collection **unlisted**: anyone with the link can view it, including people without an account, but it isn't listed anywhere public. The link shows the collection's routes; private routes inside it stay hidden from others.

## Delete a collection

Use the delete action and click again to confirm. The routes inside it stay in your library.
