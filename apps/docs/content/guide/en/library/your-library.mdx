Open **Library** from the left rail (or the bottom tab bar on a phone) to see everything you've saved. The library requires an account: signed out, you'll see a sign-in prompt instead.

The panel has two tabs: **Routes** for individual routes and **[Collections](/en/guide/library/collections)** for grouping them.

## Route cards

Each saved route is a card with a map preview, name, activity, and key stats. Click a card to open its [details](/en/guide/library/route-details). The heart on the card toggles **favourite**, and delete sits right next to it. The card menu holds the rest:

- **Edit in planner**: load the route into the plan panel for waypoint edits
- **Rename**
- **Add to collection**
- **Edit tags**
- **Export GPX**

## Search and filters

- **Search** matches route names as you type.
- **Activity chips** narrow to Cycling, Running, or Walking; the heart chip shows favourites only.
- **Filters** adds distance bands (short to epic), visibility (private, unlisted, public), and tags.
- **Sort** orders by recently updated, name, distance, or elevation gain.

## Tags

Tags are free-form labels (up to 10 per route) and the library's main organisation tool: `commute`, `gravel`, `holiday-2026`, anything you like. Add them from the card menu or the route's detail panel, then filter on them from the **Filters** menu.

## Starting fresh

An empty library offers the three ways to get a first route in: **Plan a route**, **Generate a loop**, or **Import GPX**.
