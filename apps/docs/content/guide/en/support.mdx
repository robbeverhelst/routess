## Found a bug?

Open an issue on [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/robbeverhelst/routess/issues). Include:

- What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
- Your browser and operating system
- A screenshot if the problem is visual

## Want a feature?

Feature requests go through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/robbeverhelst/routess/issues) too. Describe the problem you're trying to solve, not just the solution you have in mind.

## Questions about your data

- **Export everything**: your profile settings include a full account export (a ZIP with your data and one GPX file per route).
- **Delete your account**: see [Deleting your account](/en/guide/account/deleting-account). Deletion has a 30-day grace window during which you can change your mind by signing back in.
- **Privacy**: see [Privacy](/en/guide/privacy).

## Common problems

Check [Troubleshooting](/en/guide/troubleshooting) first; the most frequent sign-in and map issues are covered there.
