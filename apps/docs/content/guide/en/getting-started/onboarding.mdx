---
title: First-run setup
description: What the welcome screen asks and why.
---

![The routess welcome screen](/guide/welcome.jpg)

The first time you open routess it walks you through a short setup. Every answer can be changed later in **Settings**.

1. **Pick your default sport(s)**: walking, running, or cycling. The default sport drives new routes.
2. **Choose your units**: metric or imperial, applied to every distance, elevation, and pace you'll see.
3. **Your average pace**: a typical speed per sport, used to estimate route durations.
4. **Pick your map style**: Streets, Outdoors, or Satellite (see [Map styles](/en/guide/map/styles)).
5. **Allow location access?**: optional; used to center the map on you. See [Your location](/en/guide/map/your-location).

You can sign in right away or **continue without an account**; planning works either way, and [saving to your library](/en/guide/routes/saving-routes) asks you to sign in when you need it.
