## The map

Takes up most of the screen. Pan by dragging, zoom with scroll or the `+` / `-` buttons in the map toolbar.

## The left rail

The icon column on the far left switches between panels:

- **Plan**: the route you're building
- **Library**: your saved routes
- **Discover** and **Social**: shared and community routes (coming soon)
- **Settings**: sports, units, language, map and privacy options

At the bottom of the rail: notifications, the dark-mode toggle, and your account.

## The plan sidebar

Shows your current route: the activity tabs (Run, Cycle, Walk), every waypoint, the elevation and surface chart, and total route stats. Click a waypoint to focus the map on it. The **Save**, share, and export buttons live at the bottom.

## The map toolbar

Across the top of the map:

- **Search**: find a place and jump the map to it
- **Center on me**: center the map on your location (asks for permission the first time)
- **Undo / Redo**: step through your edits
- **Remove route**: clear the current route
- **Map style**, **Lock map**, **Focus on route**, and zoom `+` / `-`
