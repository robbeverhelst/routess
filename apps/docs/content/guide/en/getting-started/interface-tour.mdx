## The map

Takes up most of the screen. Pan by dragging, zoom with scroll or the `+` / `-` buttons in the bottom right.

## The route sidebar

Shows your current route: every waypoint, the distance between waypoints, and total route stats. Click a waypoint to focus the map on it.

## The top bar

- **routess logo**: back to the home view
- **Search**: find a place and jump the map to it
- **Language switcher**: change to en/nl/fr/de
- **Profile menu**: your account, sign out

## Controls

- **Undo / Redo**: step through your edits
- **Reset**: clear the current route
- **My location**: center the map on you (asks for permission the first time)
