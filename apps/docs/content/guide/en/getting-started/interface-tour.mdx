## The map

Takes up most of the screen. Pan by dragging, zoom with scroll or the `+` / `-` buttons in the map toolbar.

## The left rail

The icon column on the far left switches between panels:

- **Plan**: the route you're building
- **Library**: your [saved routes and collections](/en/guide/library/your-library)
- **Discover**: [public routes from the community](/en/guide/discover) for the area you're looking at
- **Social**: your [feed, inbox, and the people you follow](/en/guide/social/follow-and-feed)
- **Settings**: sports, units, language, map and privacy options

At the bottom of the rail: [notifications](/en/guide/social/notifications), the dark-mode toggle, and your account.

## The plan sidebar

Shows your current route: the activity tabs (Run, Cycle, Walk), the **From** and **To** endpoints with place names, the numbered waypoints in between, total route stats, and the elevation and surface chart. Click a waypoint to focus the map on it. The **Save**, share, and import buttons live at the bottom.

## The map toolbar

Across the top of the map:

- **Search**: find a place and jump the map to it
- **Generate a loop**: [round-trip suggestions](/en/guide/routes/generating-loops) from a start point and a distance
- **Center on me**: center the map on your location (asks for permission the first time)
- **Undo / Redo**: step through your edits
- **Remove route**: clear the current route
- **Map style**, **Lock map**, **Focus on route**, and zoom `+` / `-`
