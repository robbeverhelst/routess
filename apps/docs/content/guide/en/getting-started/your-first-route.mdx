Let's plan a quick route from scratch.

![The empty planner, ready for a first waypoint](/guide/planner-empty.jpg)

## 1. Click your starting point

Click anywhere on the map. A waypoint appears: that's your starting point. The first waypoint is shown in green.

## 2. Add waypoints

Click again to add the next waypoint. routess connects your waypoints with a line and shows the total distance and duration in the sidebar.

![Three waypoints connected by a routed line through Ghent](/guide/route-overview.jpg)

## 3. Reorder by dragging

Drag any waypoint to reorder it. The route updates instantly.

## 4. Undo a mistake

Made a wrong click? Hit **Undo** (or `Ctrl/Cmd + Z`). You can step backwards through every change you made.

## 5. Save your route

Routes save automatically once you're signed in. Refresh the page and your route is still there.
