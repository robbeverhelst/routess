Let's plan a quick route from scratch.

> _Screenshot placeholder: empty map after sign-in._

## 1. Click your starting point

Click anywhere on the map. A waypoint appears — that's your starting point. The first waypoint is shown in green.

## 2. Add stops

Click again to add the next stop. Routess connects your stops with a line and shows the total distance and duration in the sidebar.

> _Screenshot placeholder: 3 waypoints connected by a route line._

## 3. Reorder by dragging

Drag any waypoint to reorder it. The route updates instantly.

## 4. Undo a mistake

Made a wrong click? Hit **Undo** (or `Ctrl/Cmd + Z`). You can step backwards through every change you made.

## 5. Save your route

Routes save automatically once you're signed in. Refresh the page — your route is still there.
