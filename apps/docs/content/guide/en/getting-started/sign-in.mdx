You can sign in to routess with your Google account or with an email and password.

## Sign in with Google

1. Open routess and click **Sign in with Google** in the top right.
2. A Google popup appears. Pick the account you want to use.
3. Approve the requested permissions.
4. You're back in routess, signed in.

![The routess sign-in screen with Google, email, and anonymous options](/guide/welcome.jpg)

### What routess can see

- Your name and profile picture
- Your email address (to identify your account)

That's it. routess never reads your Gmail, Drive, or Calendar.

## Sign in with email

1. Click **Sign in with email** on the sign-in screen.
2. To create an account, choose **Create an account**, enter your email and a password, and confirm the verification link routess emails you.
3. To sign in later, enter the same email and password.

Forgot your password? Use **Send reset link** on the sign-in screen and follow the email.

If you signed up with Google, you can add a password later under your profile settings, so both methods work for the same account.

## Signing out

Open the menu in the top right and click **Sign out**. Your routes stay saved on the server and reappear next time you sign in.
