---
title: Sign in with Google
description: How to sign in to routess using your Google account.
---

routess uses Google to sign you in — there is no separate password to manage.

## Steps

1. Open routess and click **Sign in with Google** in the top right.
2. A Google popup appears. Pick the account you want to use.
3. Approve the requested permissions.
4. You're back in routess, signed in.

> _Screenshot placeholder: sign-in popup._

## What routess can see

- Your name and profile picture
- Your email address (to identify your account)

That's it. routess never reads your Gmail, Drive, or Calendar.

## Signing out

Open the menu in the top right and click **Sign out**. Your routes stay saved on the server and reappear next time you sign in.
