On a phone the map stays full-screen and the desktop side panel becomes a bottom sheet.

![The planner on a phone with the bottom tab bar](/guide/mobile-planner.jpg)

## Top bar

The bar across the top holds search, notifications, the dark-mode toggle, and your account.

## Bottom tab bar

The tabs along the bottom switch between **Plan**, **Library**, **Discover**, **Social**, and **Settings**, the same panels as the left rail on desktop. Tap the active tab again to collapse its sheet and get the full map back.

## The bottom sheet

Panels open as a sheet over the map. Drag it up for the full panel (waypoints, elevation, stats) or down to keep just a peek while the map takes the screen.

![The plan panel opened as a bottom sheet](/guide/mobile-drawer.jpg)

## Editing gestures

Route editing on touch screens has its own gesture grammar, built so the map never grabs a waypoint you didn't mean to touch:

- **Tap empty map**: add a waypoint
- **Tap a waypoint**: open its popup, with delete
- **Long-press a waypoint**: lift it (the marker grows), then drag it to a new spot
- **Long-press the route line**: insert a new waypoint there and drag it right away
- **Long-press empty map**: add a direct (straight-line) waypoint
- **Drag a lifted waypoint to the trash zone** at the bottom of the map to delete it

Moving your finger immediately always pans the map; **pinch** zooms and a **two-finger twist** rotates. To reorder waypoints in the plan sheet, press and hold a row's grip handle, then drag.
