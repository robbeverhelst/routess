## Is routess free?

Yes. routess is open source and self-hosted. The hosted version at routess.com is also free.

## Do I need an account?

You can plan a route without signing in, but routes saved while signed out live only in your browser. Sign in to keep them across sessions and devices.

## What data does routess store?

- Your name and email (from Google)
- Your saved routes (waypoints + metadata)

Nothing else. No tracking, no analytics on your routes.

## Can I export my routes?

Export to GPX/KML is on the roadmap. For now, routes live in the app only.

## Can I run my own copy?

Yes — routess is open source. See **[Developer Docs → Operations](/docs/operations/docker)**.

## Where do I report a bug?
