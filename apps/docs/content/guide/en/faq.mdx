## Is routess free?

Yes. routess is open source and self-hosted, and a self-hosted instance has every feature unlocked. The hosted version at routess.com is free for planning, GPX import and export, 3 generated routes a day when signed in, and up to 5 saved routes. Pro, a one-off year pass, adds 50 generated routes a day, unlimited saved routes, knooppunt route generation, turn-by-turn navigation, collections, and API tokens for the CLI and agents.

## Do I need an account?

No. You can plan, save and export routes without one: routes saved while signed out live in this browser on this device. Create a free account (or sign in) and they move into it, so they are on every device you sign in on. Publishing, social features and sync need an account.

## What data does routess store?

- Your name and email (from Google sign-in or email signup)
- Your saved routes (waypoints + metadata)

routess collects anonymous usage events on a self-hosted analytics instance to understand which features get used. These events never include your email, your route names, or your raw account ID. There are no third-party trackers.

## Can I export my routes?

Yes. Open a route and use **Save as GPX** in the sidebar to download it. You can also import GPX files into the planner. TCX, FIT, and KML imports are planned.

## Can I run my own copy?

Yes, routess is open source. See **[Developer Docs → Operations](/docs/operations/self-host)**.
