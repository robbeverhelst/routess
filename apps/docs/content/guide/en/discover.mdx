**Discover** shows public routes from the community for whatever part of the map you're looking at. Open it from the left rail (or the bottom tab bar on mobile). Browsing works without an account.

![Discover with community route cards for the visible map area](/guide/discover.jpg)

## The map is the search

Pan or zoom anywhere and the list refreshes to the routes in view, newest first, up to 50 at a time. There is no search button: moving the map is the query.

Two filters narrow the list:

- **Activity**: All, Cycling, Running, or Walking
- **Distance**: any distance, or a band (under 5 km, 5 to 15 km, 15 to 50 km, over 50 km)

A counter shows how many routes are in view.

## Route cards

Each result shows the route's name, activity, distance, elevation gain, a map thumbnail, the author, and where the route starts (city and region, derived automatically from the start point). Hovering a card on desktop highlights that route on the map.

Clicking a card opens the route's [public page](/en/guide/routes/sharing-routes) with the full map, stats, and a GPX download. Sign in there to save a copy into your own library.

## Getting your routes into Discover

Discover lists routes whose visibility is **public**. Make a route public from its detail panel in your [library](/en/guide/library/your-library) and it shows up for anyone browsing that area. See [sharing routes](/en/guide/routes/sharing-routes) for how visibility works; unlisted routes stay out of Discover.
