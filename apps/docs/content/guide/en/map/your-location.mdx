routess can show your location on the map and follow you as you move.

> _Screenshot placeholder: location dot on the map._

## Enable location

Click the **My location** button in the controls. Your browser will ask for permission the first time — choose **Allow**.

A blue dot appears on the map at your current position.

## Privacy

Your location stays in your browser. routess does not send your live location to its servers.

## Trouble?

- Make sure your browser is allowed to use location for this site
- HTTPS is required — locations don't work on plain HTTP
- Some VPNs and corporate networks block geolocation
