title: Display options
description: Map and display settings that change what you see.
---

Open **Settings** from the left rail and scroll to **Map & display**.

![The map and display settings](/guide/map-display.jpg)

## Map behaviour

- **Map style**: Streets, Outdoors, or Satellite (see [Map styles](/en/guide/map/styles))
- **Auto-snap to roads**: waypoints you drop or drag snap onto the nearest road
- **Off-track guide line**: shows a dashed line to the nearest point on your route when you stray off it
- **Heading cone**: draws a cone on your location marker pointing the way you're facing

## Appearance

- **Theme**: light or dark, also toggled with `Ctrl/Cmd + D`
- **Accent color**: violet, cobalt, forest, or ember

## Experimental

Under **Advanced** you'll find experimental toggles:

- **Node network overlays**: show numbered cycling and hiking node networks on the map. Common in Belgium and the Netherlands; useful for planning node-to-node tours.

Experimental features may change or disappear between releases.
