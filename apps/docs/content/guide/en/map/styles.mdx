---
title: Map styles
description: Switch between map appearances.
---

routess offers three built-in map styles. Open the **Map style** button in the map toolbar to switch.

![The map style switcher with Streets, Outdoors, and Satellite](/guide/map-styles.jpg)

## Available styles

- **Streets**: default detailed street view
- **Outdoors**: contour lines and trail detail, useful for hiking
- **Satellite**: aerial imagery

The selected style is remembered across sessions.

Looking for a dark map? Dark mode is a theme, not a map style: toggle it from the left rail or with `Ctrl/Cmd + D`.
