## Pan

Click and drag to move around. On touch devices, drag with one finger.

## Zoom

- Scroll up to zoom in, scroll down to zoom out
- Double-click to zoom in
- Pinch with two fingers on touch devices
- Use the `+` / `-` buttons in the map toolbar

## Rotate & tilt

Hold `Ctrl` (or right-click) and drag to rotate. Hold `Ctrl + Shift` to tilt the map into a 3D perspective.

## Recenter

Click the **Center on me** button in the map toolbar to recenter on your current location (the browser will ask permission the first time).

## Search

Click **Search location** in the map toolbar (or open it from the command palette) and type a place name or address; picking a result jumps the map there. Your recent searches show up underneath for quick reuse. Search history is stored only on your device, never on the server.
