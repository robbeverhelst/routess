A route is just a list of waypoints. To create one, click anywhere on the map.

- The first click sets your **starting point** (green marker).
- Each click adds a waypoint (numbered marker).
- routess draws a connecting line between them as you go.

## Tips

- **Hold and drag** when placing a marker for fine adjustments.
- **Right-click** (or long-press on touch) to remove a waypoint.
- **Click an empty area between two waypoints** to insert a waypoint in the middle.

Continue with [Editing routes](/en/guide/routes/editing-routes).
