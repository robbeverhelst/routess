The sidebar shows live stats for your route as you build it.

> _Screenshot placeholder: sidebar with distance, duration, elevation, and surface chart._

## Total stats

- **Distance**: sum of all legs, in km or mi (set in your account)
- **Duration**: estimated travel time for the sport you've selected
- **Elevation gain**: total climb across the full route

## Per-leg stats

Click a waypoint in the sidebar to expand it. You'll see the distance and duration of the leg leading into that waypoint.

## Elevation and surface chart

Under the stats, a single chart shows the elevation profile of your route with the surface type underlaid as colored bands. Hover the chart to see the elevation, distance, and surface at that point on the route. The matching point on the map highlights as you move.

The route line on the map also uses dash patterns to hint at surface: solid for paved, dashed for unpaved, dotted for trails. The chart and the map share the same color scale, so you can match one to the other at a glance.

## How estimates are calculated

Duration uses your per-sport pace from **Settings → Sports**. Each sport (walking, running, cycling, driving) has its own default pace; override the default if it doesn't match how fast you actually move. Elevation gain comes from Mapbox Terrain-RGB; surface comes from the routing engine.
