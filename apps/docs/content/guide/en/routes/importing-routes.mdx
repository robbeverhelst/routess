---
title: Importing routes
description: Bring existing GPX files into routess.
---

Open **Import** from the plan sidebar, the library, or the command palette (`Ctrl/Cmd + K`).

![The import dialog](/guide/import-route.jpg)

## How it works

1. Drop a file on the dialog or click **Choose file**.
2. routess reads the track, detects the activity type, and loads it into the planner.
3. Review it on the map, then save it to your library like any other route.

## Supported formats

**GPX** is supported today, up to 25 MB. TCX, FIT, and KML are planned; picking one shows a notice until then.

GPX exports from other apps (Strava, Komoot, Garmin Connect, etc.) import fine. The reverse also works: every route in routess can be [exported as GPX](/en/guide/routes/sharing-routes).
