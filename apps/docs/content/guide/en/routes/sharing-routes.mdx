---
title: Sharing routes
description: Share a route with a link or through native share targets.
---

Click **Share** in the route sidebar, or open a saved route and click its share button, to bring up the share modal.

![The share modal with map preview, share link, share targets, and GPX export](/guide/share-modal.jpg)

## What's in the modal

- **Copy link**: copies a link to your clipboard. The route itself is encoded in the link, so it always reflects the route as it was when you copied it.
- **Native share**: on mobile, this opens your phone's share sheet (WhatsApp, Messages, Mail, etc).
- **Share targets**: send the route directly to email, WhatsApp, Facebook, or X.
- **Export GPX**: download the route as a GPX file instead of sharing a link.
- **Preview**: a map thumbnail and the route stats, so the person you send it to knows what they're getting.

## What the recipient sees

Anyone who opens the link sees the route loaded in the planner: the path on the map, the stats, and the elevation and surface chart. They don't need an account to view it, and they can export it as GPX. To save a copy into their own library, they'll have to sign in.

## Visibility: private, unlisted, public

Saved routes have a visibility setting, chosen when you save and changeable later:

- **Private**: only you can see it in your library.
- **Unlisted**: anyone with the link can view it.
- **Public**: visible to anyone.

You can set a default for new routes under **Settings → Routing defaults → Default visibility**. Sharing a link from the share modal does not change a route's visibility, because the link carries the route data itself.
