Open a saved route and click **Share** to bring up the share modal.

> _Screenshot placeholder: share modal with link, copy, and native share options._

## What's in the modal

- **Copy link** — copies the public URL of the route to your clipboard.
- **Native share** — on mobile, this opens your phone's share sheet (WhatsApp, Messages, Mail, etc).
- **Preview** — a thumbnail and the route stats, so the person you send it to knows what they're getting.

## Public vs private

Sharing a route requires it to be **public**. If the route is private, the modal asks you to flip its visibility first, and warns you that the link becomes anyone-can-view. You can revert to private later from **Edit → Visibility**.

## What the recipient sees

Anyone with the link sees the route on the map, the stats, the elevation/surface chart, and a download as GPX button. They don't need an account to view it. To save a copy into their own library, they'll have to sign in.
