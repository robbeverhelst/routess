---
title: Routing preferences
description: Profiles and preferences that shape how routess routes you.
---

Open **Routing preferences** from the sliders icon at the top of the plan sidebar, or through the command palette (`Ctrl/Cmd + K`). Preferences are saved on your device and applied when you hit **Apply**.

## Profiles

Pick the profile that matches the ride or run you want:

- **Fastest**: the default; the most direct sensible route
- **Scenic**: trades up to ~15% extra time for nicer roads
- **Safest**: prefers bike paths and calm streets
- **Flat**: avoids hills where possible

## Fine-tuning

Below the profile you can toggle individual preferences:

- **Prefer bike infrastructure**: weigh cycle paths and lanes higher
- **Avoid steep climbs**: steer around gradients, with a **max climb gradient (%)** slider
- **Avoid unpaved**: stick to sealed surfaces
- **Avoid highways**: exclude motorway segments
- **Auto-snap waypoints**: dropped waypoints snap onto the nearest road

**Reset** restores the defaults. After **Apply**, new routes and recalculations use your settings; existing legs aren't changed until you edit them.
