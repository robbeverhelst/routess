---
title: Routing preferences
description: Surface and avoidance preferences that shape how routess routes you.
---

Open **Routing preferences** from the sliders icon at the top of the plan sidebar, or through the command palette (`Ctrl/Cmd + K`). Preferences are saved on your device and applied when you hit **Apply**.

![The routing preferences dialog](/guide/routing-preferences.jpg)

## Surface preference

Bias the route toward roads with a surface type:

- **Paved**: stick to sealed roads, good for road bikes
- **Mixed**: the default; take whatever connects best
- **Unpaved**: prefer gravel and trails

When a calculated route can't honour your preference, the route info shows a surface mismatch warning so you know before you head out.

## Avoidances

- **Avoid highways**: excludes motorway segments from routing
- **Avoid ferries**: skips routes that cross water by ferry

**Reset** restores the defaults. After **Apply**, new routes and recalculations use your settings; existing legs aren't changed until you edit them.
