## Endpoints

The sidebar starts with your **From** and **To** rows, shown as place names rather than coordinates. Click either one to replace it via search, and use the swap button between them to reverse the whole route's direction.

When your route starts and ends in the same place, the two rows collapse into a single **Loop from …** row. Not a loop yet? **Back to start** adds a final leg home in one click.

## Numbered waypoints

The waypoints between the endpoints are numbered, and the same numbers appear on the map markers, so the list and the map always line up. Each row shows the distance along the route to that point.

## Reorder

Drag a waypoint marker on the map, or drag a row by its grip handle in the sidebar. The route updates as you drop.

## Delete

Right-click a waypoint on the map (or tap it on touch screens) to remove it. The route reroutes around the gap. Row actions in the sidebar appear when you hover a row.

## Hover sync between sidebar and map

Hover a waypoint in the sidebar to highlight it on the map, and hover a marker on the map to highlight its row in the sidebar. This works in both directions, which is handy when a long route has many waypoints.

## Undo / Redo

- **Undo:** click the undo button or press `Ctrl/Cmd + Z`
- **Redo:** redo button or `Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + Z`

routess keeps your full edit history for the current session.

## Edit a saved route in place

Open a route from your library and edit its name, description, visibility, or waypoints directly. An "Unsaved changes" indicator appears next to the title while you have pending edits. Click **Save** to commit, or **Discard** to revert to the last saved version. If you navigate away with unsaved changes, routess prompts you first.

## Reset

Click **Reset** to clear the current route entirely. This is undo-able right away, useful if you cleared by accident.

Editing on a phone works differently; see [routess on your phone](/en/guide/mobile).
