---
title: Saving routes
description: How routess persists your routes across sessions.
---

The route you're working on is kept in your browser automatically: refresh the page and it's still there, even when you're not signed in.

![The route still present after a page refresh](/guide/route-after-refresh.jpg)

## Saving to your library

Click **Save** in the sidebar to store the route in your library with a name and a visibility (private, unlisted, or public). Saved routes are stored in your account and sync across devices. Saving requires an account; if you're signed out, routess asks you to sign in first.

Once saved, routes live in [your library](/en/guide/library/your-library), where you can tag them, favourite them, and group them into collections.

## Save a copy

Working from a saved route but want to keep the original? Use **Save as copy** in the plan sidebar to store your edits as a new route and leave the original untouched.

## What if I'm not signed in?

Your in-progress route lives only in your browser. It survives refreshes, but it won't follow you to another device and can be lost if you clear browser data. Sign in and save to keep it.

## Removing a route

Click **Reset** (the trash icon) to clear the current route from the map. To delete a saved route, open it from your library and use its delete action.
