---
title: Saving routes
description: How Routess persists your routes across sessions.
---

Once you're signed in, your current route is saved automatically and survives:

- Page refreshes
- Closing and re-opening the browser
- Signing in on a different device

There is no **Save** button — Routess writes changes as you make them.

> _Screenshot placeholder: route present after page refresh._

## What if I'm not signed in?

Anonymous routes live only in your browser. Sign in (with Google) to keep them.

## Removing a saved route

Click **Reset** to clear the route from the map. The next save replaces the previous one.
