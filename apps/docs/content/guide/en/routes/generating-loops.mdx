---
title: Generating loops
description: Generate round-trip route suggestions from a start point and a distance.
---

Out of inspiration? **Generate a loop** builds round trips for you: from your starting point, back to your starting point. It works without an account.

![The Generate a loop dialog with start point, target distance, direction, and surface](/guide/generate-loop.jpg)

## Generate

1. Click the compass button in the map toolbar.
2. Pick a start point: **Map center**, **My location**, or **Pick on map**.
3. Set the **Target distance** with the slider (1 to 100 km).
4. Optionally bias the **Direction** (Any, North, East, South, West) and the **Surface** (Mixed, Paved, Unpaved).
5. Click **Generate**.

Loops are generated for the activity you have selected (Run, Cycle, Walk) and follow your [routing preferences](/en/guide/routes/routing-preferences).

## Pick a loop

After a few moments, up to three loops appear on the map, each with a card showing distance, duration, elevation gain, and the surface breakdown. A loop that mostly retraces the same roads gets a "repeated roads" warning on its card.

- Click a card, or a dimmed loop on the map, to preview it.
- **Use this route** loads the loop into the plan, where you can [edit](/en/guide/routes/editing-routes) and [save](/en/guide/routes/saving-routes) it like any other route.
- **Show different loops** regenerates with shapes in directions you haven't seen yet.

## When nothing comes back

Some spots just don't loop well. If generation fails, routess says why and offers one-click fixes: try any direction, try a shorter distance, or start over. If the start point isn't near a road or path, move it closer and generate again.
