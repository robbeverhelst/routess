# Title in imperative voice

One paragraph: what we decided, the constraint that forced the decision, and the trade-off we accepted. A future reader should be able to stop here and know the gist.

## Considered options

- **Option A** — chosen because …
- **Option B** — rejected because …
- **Option C** — rejected because …

## Consequences

- Positive: what becomes easier or safer.
- Negative: what becomes harder or more expensive.
- Follow-ups: TODOs this decision creates.

## References

- Issues, PRs, prior art, or external docs informing the decision.
